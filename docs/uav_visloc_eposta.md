**Subject:** UAV-VisLoc — releasing derived metadata and model weights (research use)

Dear Dr. Xu and co-authors,

We are an undergraduate research team in Türkiye working on a TÜBİTAK 2209-A project on reliable UAV-to-satellite
localization. UAV-VisLoc was our main benchmark; thank you for making it available.

We would like to open-source our code (MIT) together with a small release package, and want to make sure this is
in line with your intentions, since we could not find a license for the dataset:

- **No images** from UAV-VisLoc (no drone frames, satellite maps or crops) — users download the dataset from your page.
- **Derived metadata:** lists of your file names with our region split, the tile geometry we computed for each pair,
  and heading-label corrections.
- **Model weights** of a small CNN (≈ 52 MB × 3) trained from scratch on regions 01, 08, 09 and 10.
- Everything marked "research use only" and citing your paper.

Would this be acceptable to you? If you prefer different terms, or want us to leave out the weights, we will follow
your preference.
